Decomposition:
- **Not unconditional sigma error.** Over all 9 settled hours per asset (replica at T-3.0 vs `expiration_value`), rms z_real = 0.96 BTC / 0.81 ETH / 1.15 SOL vs the model's 0.92 (t8, scale .8); mean z_real ≈ 0. EWMA-0.94 sigma is calibrated on average.
- **Conditional sigma error = selection.** The model only trades when it disagrees with the mid, and the mid's implied sigma was right every time: when σ_mkt > σ_model (BTC 05/09/10Z) the realized |z_real| was 1.3–1.7 (big moves, our favourite-NO lost); when σ_mkt < σ_model (7 trades) |z_real| was 0.16–0.82 (small moves, our longshot lost). 11/11 ordered correctly (binomial p ≈ 0.001 under "no information in the mid's sigma"). The mid carries intra-hour/order-flow vol information that a 1-min-candle EWMA cannot.
- **Student-t favourite thesis (fatter tails ⇒ favourites underpriced)**: 7 of 11 trades were longshots *against* a 0.89–0.95 favourite (p_t shades favourites toward 0.5 by 10–20 pts relative to the mid). All 7 lost. That is the opposite of the thesis in action: scale .8 tightens the core only for |z| < ~1; at |z| 0.5–0.9 the t8/.8 cdf is *flatter* than the mid.
- **Variance share**: the three "right side, lost anyway" trades (BTC 05/09/10Z, −14.4 of −20.2 $) are 1.3–1.7σ moves — plausible variance individually, but the market priced them (σ_mkt 3.6–12.9 vs 2.4–2.9).
- Drift: BTC 79.5k → 80.2k over 05–13Z; 9 of 10 NO bets lost to YES. The replica −0.4 bp basis adds ~0.1σ in the same direction. Minor.

**Posterior on P10 BTC edge** (prior N(+10.8, 4.6²) c from the hindcast; data mean −28.0, cl.se 19.3, n=6): posterior **+8.7 ± 4.5 c**. Pooling all 11 P10 trades (mean −22.4, se 11.5): +6.2 ± 4.3. By the P&L criterion the thesis is *not* rejectable at this n (per-contract sd 47c ⇒ ~90 trades for a 5c se). But the pre-registered secondary gate — model Brier < mid Brier — is failing at 155 rows (+.0102 ± .0084, model worse), and the mechanism test above is 11/11 against. P10's own pause rule (CI upper < 0 at n ≥ 60) is far from triggering; the honest statement is "the model is dominated by the mid on every measure we can see; the prior came from a hindcast whose in-sample sigma selection P10 itself removed".
