## P18 — Thompson lever selection for the final-minute rule (registered 2026-10-27 19:25 PT; docs/TS_LEVERS_2026-10-27.md; code src/predhft/ts_levers.py; P18_T0 = first close after the three s7p13-KX*15M runners restart with --ts-levers)
- Hypothesis: at least one setting of {edge 3/5/7c, timing early T-55..T-30 / mid T-40..T-10 / late T-25..T-10, cap .95/.97, order take/join} of the P16 rule has mean net per contract > 0; the four levers interact, so 36 full-factorial arms are evaluated by counterfactual replay of every settled 15M window (full-information update for all arms every close; Thompson sampling chooses only the single live paper bet and reports P(best)). Prior per arm N(0, 10c) on the mean (market calibrated); noise sd pooled per arm, shrunk to 35c, floor 15c; cluster = settlement close (mean over the assets the arm traded). Paper only. risk.size remains the ceiling; one bet per close unchanged.
- Bootstrap (in-sample, 216 asset-windows / 75 closes, 0.85 d): no arm has n >= 8 closes; reference rule -10.1 +- 12.9 c/ct; best take arm E7-early-C95-take -0.1 +- 12.4. These numbers are a prior, not a result.
- n_required: an arm may be *selected on its own posterior* only after n >= 30 closes (before that its selection probability is capped at the reference arm's / uniform; it still updates every close). Evaluation of the winner: >= 60 closes AND posterior P(mu > 0) >= 0.975 AND raw mean - 1.96 se_cl > 0 for the same arm, measured on closes >= P18_T0 only (bootstrap closes excluded from the pass test). Then that arm becomes the registered fixed rule (new PREREG entry); sizing per the EDGE_PLAN ladder.
- Guard (sampling floor): an arm with posterior mean < 0 after n >= 40 closes keeps selection probability >= 2% (never zero). Why: the posterior comes from a replay whose fill model (take: worse of two asks + 1 tick; join: strict crossing) is itself a hypothesis; only live-selected arms produce real fills to check it, and the paper cost of a 2% draw on a -5c arm is ~0.1c per close. Join arms are never selected live (the paper runner cannot rest orders); they are replay-scored only.
- Size lever is derived, not sampled: full risk.size when the chosen arm's P(mu > 0) >= 0.75 and n >= 30, else 0.5x (min_lot floor). Hour filter is a stratified report (all / US-day 06-16 PT / high-vol PT hours frozen in state), not an arm; a bucket may become a lever only with >= 40 opportunities in that bucket and a registered amendment. Candidate arms E{3,5,7}-{early,mid}-C95-take-F50 (buy only when our side is also the market favourite, price >= .50) were added after seeing the bootstrap; they are replayed and reported but excluded from selection until they have >= 30 closes after P18_T0.
- Stop rule: if after 300 closes (>= P18_T0) no arm has posterior P(mu > 0) >= 0.9, or the arm with the highest P(best) has raw mean - 1.96 se_cl < 0 at n >= 60, the lever search closes: P16 goes scored-only and the next step is model (p_cal) work, not lever work. Arm definitions, prior and guard constants are frozen at P18_T0; changes need a new entry.
