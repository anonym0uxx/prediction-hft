# S7 crypto-hourly redesign — participation, sigma, maker, pricing, rule (2026-10-26)

Author: principal-quant subagent. All numbers below computed tonight from `data/s7/*/rows.json`, Coinbase 1-min OHLC (api.exchange.coinbase.com, keyed by bucket END), Kalshi 1-min candlesticks re-fetched for the 525 near-money KXBTCD tickers (72 KXETHD), `data/s7live*/books.jsonl`, `data/replica/BTC.jsonl`, `data/s7final-KXBTCD/*`. Scripts were throwaway under `$TMPDIR/s7r/` (sigma_eval.py, partic.py, compo.py, regime_t.py, loo.py, maker.py, dyn.py, live_rep.py); outputs are quoted verbatim. No repo code modified. se = hour-clustered (each settlement hour is one observation) unless stated. "c" = cents per contract, 10-lot taker fee, +1 tick slippage where stated.

**Bottom line (read this first).**
1. We are not "not trading". Ungated P1 took one position in every BTC hour that had a signal (20:52Z-01:55Z: 6 of 6 hours). The gated P6 run was 1.5 h long and hit two sigma-hi hours; that is the whole "0 trades" observation. Ungated expected rate at T-3/thr .05 is 0.87 signals/hr, 79/130 hours have >=1 → ~15 trades/day BTC at one-per-hour; the gate removes ~50% of hours → ~7/day.
2. The signals we do take are the wrong ones. Normal + trailing-120 sigma over-prices OTM strikes: live and hindcast trades are longshots at 5-28c with 21-25% hit rates. Realized settlement moves are leptokurtic with a tighter core: Student-t fits are fold-stable at nu 5-8, scale 0.7-0.9 across 7 leave-one-day-out folds, flip the trade mix to favourites (hit 45-51%) and improve Brier vs mid at T-3 (BTC: -5.2±3.8 x1e-3).
3. **No rule reaches 95% after honest multiplicity.** Best BTC cell (T-3, EWMA-0.94 sigma, t(8, 0.8), taker +1 tick, no gate): +10.8c ±4.6 LOO, n=64, 0.49/hr — but it is the max of ~60 cells and the same recipe loses on ETH (-2.9c ±6.6, n=33). Maker posting saves ~1.5c fee but adverse selection costs 5-8c; net +2..+6c ±3.5-4, not better than taker at T-3, better at T-5.
4. The sigma gate is not the fix: with an out-of-sample regime definition (rolling 48-h median) the lo/hi gap halves (trail120 T-3: lo +4.3 vs hi +1.2, from +12.0 vs -5.7 with the in-sample 4.39 threshold). Replace the gate by (a) the t pricing, (b) a threshold that scales with sigma uncertainty and spread.
5. The replica's "2.4 bp/sqrt-min at 1 s" is a smoothing artifact (1-s autocorr 0.19, VR(60s/1s)=3.2); at 60 s the replica sigma is 4.16 vs Coinbase 4.35 bp/sqrt-min. Do **not** lower final-minute sigma.
6. Honest answer on EV at retail size: expected after shrinkage ~+3..+5c/contract on ~10 BTC trades/day at lot 10 = ~$3-5/day, with a 50-day pre-registered test needed to prove it. ETH/SOL: scored only. If that is not worth the operational cost, the correct decision is to stop S7 taker and keep it as a scored family.

## 2. Maker variant (Kalshi 1-min candle paths, 525 KXBTCD near-money tickers, 130 hours)

Method: decide at T-h from the candle closing at T-h (bid/ask) and Coinbase spot at T-h; post on the side p_model favours at q = floor(p_side − margin) (never crossing: q ≤ ask_side − 1 tick; skip if q < .02 or > 10c behind the bid), or "touch+1" = bid_side + 1 tick if p_side − q ≥ thr; one order per asset-hour (max (p−q)/√(p(1−p))); fill iff a later minute (≤ T-1, or ≤ T for HOLD) has volume > 0 and the trade price range goes **strictly through** q; maker fee 0; hold to settlement. Taker comparator on the same tickers: ask + 1 tick, 10-lot fee. "hyp unfilled" = what unfilled orders would have made had they filled at q (adverse-selection gauge).

Findings:
- Fill rate 64-82% at p−.02, 50-73% at p−.04; 58/65 fills occur in the first minute after posting (candle ranges are several cents wide at 10k+ contracts/min). The maker is effectively a cheaper taker, not a spread-capturer. Fill realism is optimistic (no queue, 1-min bars) and the strict-through condition only partly offsets.
- Adverse selection is real and larger than the fee saved: unfilled orders would have made +11..+15c vs +2..+6c on filled (trail120), +5..+11 vs +4..+7 (ew94/t). The fee saved is ~1.5c at 25c prices.
- Maker beats taker at T-5 (+6.0±3.5 vs +0.9±4.1; +6.1±3.8 vs +1.6±6.4) where the model has no taker edge; at T-3 with the t model taker beats maker (+11.4 vs +6.8). Posting more strikes (all strikes, 1.28 fills/hr) dilutes to +1.5±3.1.
- HOLD through the final minute changes nothing (1 extra fill) — resting orders are not systematically picked off in the last minute at these margins, contrary to the rules-card worry; but the sample is one week.

**Maker verdict:** a T-5 "post at p−.02, cancel at T-3" leg roughly doubles fills/hr vs the T-3 taker with similar EV (+6±3.5c); it is not a path to significance on its own. Carry it as a scored family (section 4), not as the primary.

Expected numbers (BTC): 0.40-0.49 trades/hr → **~10-12 trades/day**; hindcast +11..+15c/contract (max-of-many; shrunk prior +3..+5c); hour-of-day buckets all positive (US eve 21-03Z +11.8 n=17, US day +4.6 n=26, Asia/EU +7.5 n=23). Pre-registered pass criteria: n_required = (1.96·38/edge)² → **edge 5c: 222 trade-hours (~20 days); edge 3c: 616 (~55 days)**. Pass = mean net > 0 at 95% hour-clustered AND model Brier < mid Brier on near-money T-3 snaps at 95%. Pause at n ≥ 60 if the 95% CI upper bound < 0. No parameter (λ, nu, scale, thr formula, window) may change before n_required. Sizing per EDGE_PLAN C1-C5 (shrunk edge τ=3c, k ≤ 0.125 until 2x n_required).
