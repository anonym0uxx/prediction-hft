## 4. Exposure-cap design

Current: `risk.CFG["max_cost_per_settlement_ts"]` = $40 (was $20) summed over **all families** and **both sides of the same ticker** within ±60 s of close_ts (`Ledger.cost_settlement`). At :15/:30/:45 six families share the close (3×P12 + 3×P13), at :00 seven (+BTCD). Worst case independent-fill demand at lot 10: 6×$9.9 = $59 (+$9.9 at :00) → the $40 cap still blocks the 5th fill, and it counts a P12 YES and a P13 NO on the same ticker as $2 of risk when the pair's loss-at-risk is the net.

What the cap is for: P13's loss mode is a joint late reversal when |d| is small in several assets at once; 1-min BTC/ETH/SOL returns are correlated (≈0.5–0.7), so three simultaneous 0.9-priced fills can lose 3×$9 = $27 = 11% of bankroll in one window. Measured P(loss | |d|≥1 bp) ≈ 2% per asset (3/142 snapshots, 1/11 windows); the joint tail is what matters, not the sum of costs.

Proposed structure (all in `risk.py`, no rule change):

| cap | value | why |
|---|---|---|
| per (close_ts, ticker) **net** exposure | max(YES cost, NO cost) per ticker, not the sum | opposite sides on one market hedge; the ETH 14:30 P12 YES $3.80 should not block the P13 NO |
| per (close_ts, rule group) | P13 group = 3 × `max_cost_per_market`-at-lot = **$30** (lot 10 × ≤0.99 × 3 assets); P12 group separate $30 | each experiment can fill all its assets independently; correlation inside a group is what the daily family loss cap ($15) and the P13 pause rule (hit<70% at n≥20) address |
| global per close_ts | **$60** (sum of the two groups) | keeps a hard ceiling for the :00 BTCD overlap |
| `thin_fraction` | keep 0.5 below 50 resting | matched the SOL fill (22.5 resting → 10) |

## 6. What the 24%-stale and 69%-pinned numbers say about the edge

The rule only has inventory to buy when the market lags the TWAP projection by ≥5 c for ≥1 s. That happened in 11 of 98 windows, 8 of them within 5 s of T-55, and the tape shows the lag is created by spot-chasing takers sweeping the book the wrong way (SOL 0.47 at d=+2.9 bp; ETH YES 0.14 at d=−6.5 bp). Everything in §5 is about being in the book during those seconds with a free limiter slot and no cap in the way; nothing in §5 changes what we buy or at what belief.
