## 0. Verdict in six lines

1. **The overnight profit was not an outlier — and neither was the afternoon loss.** Both are ordinary draws from a zero-edge process: pooled P12+P13 (31 trades) won 19 times vs **18.8 expected from the market price** (z = 0.10) and 24.1 expected from our model. Cluster-bootstrap under the pooled distribution: P(7 hour-clusters ≥ +$11.38) = 0.21, P(5 clusters ≤ −$26.58) = 0.15. Overnight−day difference in c/ct: +19.7 ± 16.1 pooled (t = 1.2), +34 ± 16 for P12 (t = 2.2, 12 clusters, confounded 1:1 with US-session and with one 4-trade close). "Re-base on the overnight" is the wrong frame: the whole tape says **the market is calibrated and our model is not.**
2. **Root cause of the day's −$24.44 (94% of the day's loss) at the 18:30Z close:** four same-direction bets (P12+P13 × BTC+ETH) on a 1.5–2 bp projection margin whose realised one-sigma error at T-55 is **2.35 bp** (measured on 163 windows). ETH was lost to a **basis excursion** (+1.67 bp vs the +0.70 assumed, 2.9σ, same sign in all 3 assets for 3 consecutive closes 18:00–18:30Z: our 4-of-7-venue replica missed a venue premium); BTC was lost to **path reversal** (spot went −2.0 → +1.9 bp in T-45..T-35, the 60-s TWAP finished +0.1 bp above K on our own replica, +1.9 bp officially). Not liquidity, not latency, not a cross-asset shock (BTC vol that hour 2.6 bp/min, the lowest of the US session).
3. **The belief floor 0.97 is falsified.** Window-level sign accuracy of the projection at T-50..60 with |d| 1–2 bp is 15/18 (CI 0.60–0.94); 2–3 bp 12/14 (0.60–0.96). The 23 trades the P13 rule takes over all 165 windows were bought at mean 0.838 (break-even hit 0.846) and hit 0.783; realised P&L **−$14.61, −6.4 ± 10.9 c/ct**. No |d| gate, z-gate, window or ask-cap variant gets a CI above zero (§2.4). The 10/10 and 12/12 overnight bands that justified the floor were 10–12 windows.
4. **P12 is the same story:** mean model p_side 0.746 at mean price 0.577, realised hit 0.60; final-minute Brier when the market is contested (.05–.95): model 0.240 vs market 0.134 (425 rows); when the model is extreme (<.03/>.97) and the market is .10–.90 the model is right **24/47 = 51%** (17 windows). Student-t scale 0.8 shrinks the final-minute sd when the data wants it **×1.5 wider** (§2.3).
5. **Sizing:** same-close correlation of the projection error is 0.71–0.80, of the 50-s replica move 0.82–0.90, realised win-phi of P12/P13 pairs 0.58 (25 pairs, 100% same side). Three lot-10 bets per close carry 1.2 independent bets' worth of EV and 2.5 bets' worth of sd; the 11:20 PT change from $15 to $30 per crypto close ("Sharpe-neutral, EV×3") was wrong on both counts. With a calibrated p the Kelly stake is **zero**; with any p the per-close cap must be ≈ one lot-10 bet (§4).
6. **What is a barrier and what is a refit.** Barrier/bug (fix now, no PREREG needed): (a) the replica is not the settlement feed — Kalshi publishes it (`cfbenchmarks_value` with `avg_60s_data`); (b) the 0.97 floor and the `scale=0.8` tails are falsified parameters, not tunable ones; (c) P12 fires at T-59 before P13 at T-55 and bypasses `p13_holds_ticker`; (d) per-close crypto cap. Refit (needs a new pre-registration, refused today on 23 trades): any new D_MIN/z/entry-time choice, hour-of-day or vol sizing. **All crypto final-minute families go scored-only until the official feed is in and the calibrated test below is pre-registered.**

## 4. Sizing: same-close correlation and the Kelly haircut

Measured on the full tape:

|---|---|---|---|
| corr of projection error at T-50 (53/51/51 closes) | 0.71 | 0.74 | 0.80 |

Per-close P&L of P12+P13: single-trade closes sd $1.89, multi-trade closes sd $9.94; worst close −$24.44 (4 trades). With pairwise ρ ≈ 0.75, n same-direction bets have variance n(1+(n−1)ρ)σ² → effective independent bets n_eff = n/(1+(n−1)ρ): **2 bets → 1.14, 3 bets → 1.20, 4 → 1.23.** EV scales with n, sd with √(n(1+(n−1)ρ)): Sharpe ×1.07 (n=2), ×1.10 (n=3) — not ×1 and not "EV×3 for free"; and at EV ≤ 0 it multiplies the loss. Kelly per bet under correlation: f_n = f_1 / (1+(n−1)ρ) → each of three bets gets 0.40 f_1, total 1.2 f_1.

Kelly at calibrated beliefs: P13 BTC 18:30 was sized "kelly=98" from p = 0.97 at c = 0.56 + fee; at the market-calibrated p ≈ 0.485–0.515 Kelly = **0**. Even granting a 3-pt edge (p = c + 0.03) at c = 0.80, quarter-Kelly on $244 = 0.25·0.03/0.20·244/0.80 ≈ **11 contracts for one bet, 4–5 each for three correlated bets — below `min_lot` 5.** The arithmetic says one bet per close.

Recommended cap structure (all in `risk.CFG`, values):
- `max_cost_per_settlement_ts_crypto`: **$12** (one lot-10 at ≤ 0.95 + fee + 1 tick; was $30, was $15 before 11:20 PT). Counterfactual on the live tape, first fill per close: overnight +13.19 (11 trades), day **−9.07** (6), total **+4.12 vs −15.20**; per-close sd 6.57 → 2.20.
- `max_cost_per_settlement_ts_group` (P12 or P13 across assets): **$12** — same number; the group cap is redundant once the crypto cap is one bet.
- Same-ticker rule: symmetric. Today's `p13_holds_ticker` only stops P12 when P13 already holds; P12 filled at T-58/59 (before P13's T-55 start) on both losers, so P13 then doubled the same side. Rule: a ticker held by any s7p* family blocks every other s7p* family on that ticker, both sides.
- Keep `global_daily_loss` $25 and `family_daily_loss` $15 enforced in paper too (shadow halts fired at 21:29Z after the damage; paper_mode is for data collection, but a halt that cannot stop a family is not a halt). Per-close: the crypto cap above is the actual tail control; with it the 18:30 close costs −$3.87, not −$24.44.
